Pipeline Studio runs the human steps: the grill, the reuse check, the metric view for the gap, data quality, plan approval and PR review. Genie Code builds the assets, started from a Lakeflow Job whose prompt @-mentions the skills. Two human gates: approve the plan, then approve the PR. Then promote dev, staging, prod.
